About Me

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Stony Brook University, working in the Visual Analytics and Imaging Lab with Prof. Klaus Mueller. I build explainable and trustworthy AI for medical imaging, turning advanced models into transparent, controllable, and clinically reliable systems.

My research combines deep learning, computer vision, diffusion models, manifold learning, topological data analysis, and scientific visualization. I currently focus on controllable 3D lung CT synthesis and the geometric analysis of representations learned from real and synthetic medical data.

Previously, I analyzed neural-network loss landscapes at the Indian Institute of Science and developed unsupervised manifold-segmentation methods at the Indian Statistical Institute. I hold a B.Tech. in Information Technology from the University of Calcutta and enjoy translating rigorous AI research into interpretable, dependable, real-world solutions.

Research Snapshot

I develop explainable and trustworthy AI systems for medical imaging, focusing on interpretable deep learning, medical image synthesis, and visualization. My work aims to bridge advanced AI research with clinically reliable decision-making to support real-world healthcare applications.
